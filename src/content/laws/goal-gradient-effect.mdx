<Section id="plain" title="In plain words">
<Analogy>
Runners often speed up when they see the finish line, even though they’re more tired than at the start.

Seeing the end makes the last stretch feel worth it.
</Analogy>

Motivation grows as the goal gets closer. Show people **how close they are**, and they’ll try harder to finish. Give them a little **head start**, and they’ll feel closer from the beginning.
</Section>

<Section id="why" title="Why it happens">
In 1932, behaviorist Clark Hull noticed that rats in a maze ran faster the closer they got to the food. Decades later, researchers tested it with people using coffee loyalty cards: one card needed 10 stamps, the other needed 12 but came with 2 already stamped. Both needed 10 purchases, but people with the **pre-stamped card finished faster**.

Progress feels motivating, and the feeling of progress matters even when the real distance is the same.

<div class="callout">
  <strong>Break long journeys into steps.</strong> A single huge goal feels far away. Smaller milestones mean people are always “close” to the next one.
</div>
</Section>

<Section id="examples" title="Real-world examples">
<ul class="examples">
  <li><strong>Starbucks Rewards</strong><p>The app shows your stars filling up toward the next reward, so you can see how close the next free drink is.</p></li>
  <li><strong>LinkedIn profile progress</strong><p>You start at a partly complete profile, which makes finishing feel within reach.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Apple Watch rings</strong><p>The rings fill up throughout the day, and people often take a late walk just to close them.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Checkout steps</strong><p>Amazon and most online shops show “Step 3 of 4” so customers know the end is near.</p></li>
</ul>
</Section>

<Section id="apply" title="How to apply it">
<Checklist>
- **Show progress** with bars, steps, rings or percentages.
- **Give a head start.** Count steps people have already done, like creating an account.
- **Split big goals into milestones**, so the next win is always close.
- **Celebrate getting close**, with messages like “Almost there!” or “1 more step.”
- **Keep it honest.** Don’t fake progress that isn’t real, or trust will drop.
</Checklist>
</Section>
