# Code of Conduct

Laws of UX Beyond is a learning project. People arrive here at every level, from their first week in design to many years in the field. We want all of them to feel welcome.

## Our standards

**Do:**

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Every question is a fair question.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Credit other people’s ideas and work.

**Don’t:**

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Make discriminatory jokes or comments about anyone’s identity or background.
- Share other people’s private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies in the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in the UXigo Discord when you’re talking about this project.

## Reporting

If someone makes you feel unsafe or unwelcome, contact the maintainer, Irtiza Khan Labib, through [irtizakhanlabib.vercel.app](https://irtizakhanlabib.vercel.app/) or a direct message on the UXigo Discord. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block people who break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
